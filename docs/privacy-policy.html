Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

ChargeRoute ("the app") is an EV route-planning app that helps you find charging stops along your route. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and who it's shared with.

Data we collect

Location. ChargeRoute uses your device's precise or approximate location for two purposes only:

  • Planning a route starting from where you are
  • Finding charging stations near you on the map

Location is used per-request to power these features. The app does not store a history of your location, and there is no account or server that retains it.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, payment information, or any other personal information. There is no account system — ChargeRoute doesn't ask you to sign up or log in.

Who we share data with

When you use route planning or the charger map, your location and route details are sent to:

  • Google Maps Platform (Directions, Places, and Geocoding APIs) — to calculate routes, search locations, and display maps
  • Open Charge Map (openchargemap.org) — to look up EV charging station locations near your route or current position

These requests happen over HTTPS (encrypted in transit). Location data sent to these services is used only to fulfill the request (e.g. "chargers near this point") — ChargeRoute does not sell or share your data for advertising, and does not use it for any purpose beyond providing the app's features.

Google's and Open Charge Map's own privacy policies govern how they handle requests sent to their services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Open Charge Map Privacy Policy

Data stored on your device

ChargeRoute caches charger locations and vehicle specifications locally on your device (using a local database) so the app stays fast and works with a spotty connection. This cached data never leaves your device and is deleted automatically when it expires or when you uninstall the app.

Advertising and analytics

ChargeRoute does not show ads and does not use any advertising or analytics SDKs. No data is collected for advertising purposes.

Data deletion

Because ChargeRoute has no account system and stores no data on a server, there is nothing to request deletion of beyond your device's local cache — uninstalling the app removes all locally cached data.

Children's privacy

ChargeRoute is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
